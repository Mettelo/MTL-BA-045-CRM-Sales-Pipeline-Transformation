# Raw procurement and option evidence
**Mettelo BA-045 · Stage 3 · Fictional commercial correspondence, 16–18 July**
No supplier is selected. Names, offers and claims are invented. This pack is not a scored comparison, complete total-cost model or recommendation.

## SalesCloud A
Quoted setup £38,000 and annual subscription £16,000 for 20 named seats, two-year minimum. Supplier claims mobile pipeline, configurable fields, standard CSV import and quick implementation after agreed scope.

Excluded: indicative ERP connector £18,000, data cleansing/migration beyond standard import, internal backfill, training, assurance and recoverable VAT. Connector support costs are not fully stated. Offline operation, deletion audit, source-history preservation and restricted-note access were not demonstrated.

Supplier says four-week configuration assumes clean data and available owners. No evidence supports a guaranteed business benefit; any performance claim needs a comparable basis. The date is not a committed end-to-end launch.

## Suite B
Quoted setup £62,000 and £24,000/year for 25 seats, three-year minimum. Claims sales/marketing/service features and a standard ERP connection, but no interface mapping, recovery commitment or fixed migration scope is attached.

Six-week implementation assumes clean accounts and business-owner availability. Annual cost exceeds the initial £18,000 ceiling. That is relevant evidence, not a complete option judgement; capability, exclusions, sequencing and ownership still require analysis.

## Existing-platform partner proposal C
Discovery/configuration £20,000 plus £6,000 annual additional support, estimated eight weeks. Existing licences would be retained. Indicative interface work £8,000 excluded. Proposal initially covers existing account/order references and a shared workflow; broader sales-module suitability is unassessed at this stage.

Mobile usability, attribution, audit, data/access review and internal capacity are not proven. An internal option is not free, and a low quote is not a guaranteed sufficient solution. The later ERP notice adds new evidence rather than retrospectively making this source complete.

## Terms common to the source offers
The figures exclude recoverable VAT and items explicitly listed. Separate one-off, recurring, contract-term, internal effort, contingency and unknown charges. Do not count an existing commitment twice. No supplier has granted a benefit guarantee, approved data use or owner sign-off.

A contains a connector allowance; it must not be added again as a new identical charge when later delivery details arrive. B's standard connector is a claim pending validation. C's lower recurring figure does not establish workload or adoption.

## Decision use
Ask focused clarification where an exclusion affects the decision. Preserve original offers and any later changes. Do not invent missing scores, weights, migration facts or negotiated discounts. Participants must create their own credible options, cost model, comparison and recommendation with traceability to business need and constraints.
