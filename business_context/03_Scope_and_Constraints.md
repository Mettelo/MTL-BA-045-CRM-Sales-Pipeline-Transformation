# Scope and constraints

Lead capture, qualification, opportunity management, sales-to-order handover and customer visibility. Include data quality, consent/preference handling, forecast definitions and adoption. Exclude ERP replacement, automated marketing campaigns and revenue-recognition policy changes.

ERP owns orders/invoices and cannot be replaced. No new marketing contact permission may be inferred from an imported lead. Sales can spend two hours per week on discovery and training. Personal inbox migration needs scope and data review.

No production access is provided. Staff can attend one 45-minute workshop each week. Finance needs one-off and recurring costs separately, with assumptions about effort and adoption. Any change to policy requires the named policy owner; funding approval alone does not approve a control exception.
