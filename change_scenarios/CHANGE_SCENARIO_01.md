# Change notice 01 — Funding falls while the campaign date remains
**Mettelo BA-045 · Stage 5 · Raised 3 August 2026 by Emma Clarke and Sophie White**
Release after a saved pre-change baseline and the preceding notice response. This is source evidence, not a completed impact assessment.

## Trigger and changed information
One-off funding falls from £80,000 to £56,000, including £6,000 already committed. Uncommitted capacity is therefore £50,000, not £56,000 plus the commitment. The annual incremental ceiling remains £18,000. No vendor is selected.

A desired 12 October operational pilot is a planning objective, not approval to ignore a control, invent capacity or assume an uncontracted capability. This notice does not choose the participant's solution. Assess its effect on your own saved baseline.

## Supporting evidence
Supplier inputs retain excluded interfaces, migration, training and assurance. Internal configuration has cost and capacity dependencies. No approved extra revenue, cash saving or negotiated discount is supplied.

Keep earlier events and later proposals/effective dates distinguishable. No complete future requirements, option score, test plan or impact analysis is attached. Record what is established, disputed and unavailable.

## Stakeholder reactions
Sales wants the customer-facing capability preserved; Marketing wants source history; Finance rejects hiding recurring cost; IT asks for a realistic current-tools scope; Service warns against cutting handover while preserving a dashboard.

The meeting records disagreement, not consensus. Funding sponsorship does not establish every policy, technical, risk or data decision. A stakeholder preference must be validated and assessed for conflict, feasibility and authority.

## Decision required
By 5 August bring complete-cost, scope, priority and sequencing alternatives and the decision needed. The date does not approve omitting controls or assuming supplier exclusions are free.

The BA should bring a reasoned request with alternatives, cost/scope/timing implications, evidence and uncertainty. Conditional progress, escalation or deferral may be appropriate. Do not silently accept every request to preserve a date.

## Reassessment and record
Review your scope, current/future process and exceptions, requirements/rules, priorities, options/costs, controls, information/system dependencies, transition, testing and benefits assumptions. An unchanged item still needs a reasoned disposition when materially challenged.

Retain the prior version and connect the notice to affected participant artefacts, owner decisions and the new baseline. Distinguish an assumption, a request, a review comment and approval. Do not fabricate stakeholder sign-off or successful testing. The source supplies the event; the participant supplies the analysis and recommendation.
