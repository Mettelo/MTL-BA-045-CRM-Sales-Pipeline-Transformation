# MTL-BA-045 — commissioned assignment

**Mettelo Business Analyst Project Studio · distinct pathway edition**

## Business problem and reason for commissioning the team

Lead and opportunity information lives in shared sheets, personal workbooks and other systems. Sellers need quick follow-up, Marketing wants useful attribution, Finance questions forecasts and Service needs reliable won-order handover. A large CRM purchase could absorb the budget without delivering the first useful capability.

The business needs a coherent minimum viable product (MVP), not an undifferentiated list of every department’s request. Agile product analysis must show whose problem a release solves, what user behaviour creates value and how each included story can be accepted.

Read the [organisation and sponsor context](../business_context/BUSINESS_CONTEXT_AND_SPONSOR_BRIEF.md) for the observed operating environment.

## Role and business decision
You are the Agile Product Business Analyst. What smallest useful CRM capability should Beaconbridge prioritise for six sellers, and what evidence and acceptance conditions make that release credible?

A coherent, prioritised MVP product backlog and release recommendation, ready for refinement. No complete FRD, enterprise integration specification or implemented CRM.

## Business situation and intended value

After a visit, a seller wants to capture the next action on a phone without completing a long form. The lead may belong to a branch of an existing legal customer. Marketing wants campaign context, the sales manager wants a meaningful forecast and Service should only act on an accepted commitment. The team must explore a small usable slice while resolving data/permission uncertainty; no approved pipeline or story solution is supplied.

Product decision-makers can understand the release goal, follow a realistic user journey through included stories, test their acceptance conditions and see why other requests are deferred. Prototype feedback and unresolved research are represented honestly.

## Scope

Lead capture/follow-up, relevant account/site context, opportunity progression, forecast inputs and sales-to-service handover to the extent justified for the chosen MVP. Consider existing ERP capability, data-use decisions and seller adoption as constraints.

No full FRD, enterprise data-platform design, complete API specification, production CRM implementation, bulk contact import, live marketing or approved revenue policy redesign. A prototype may be low fidelity; coding is not required.

## Working agreement
Use supplied facts, conflicting stakeholder accounts and synthetic source records. Make calculations reproducible; distinguish evidence, interpretation, proposal and unresolved approval. Reference actual source paths and row/case IDs. Do not invent stakeholder sign-off, real research, executed tests or realised benefits. External references supply context, not automatic approval of your proposals.
