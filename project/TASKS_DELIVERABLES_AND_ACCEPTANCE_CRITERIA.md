# MTL-BA-045 — tasks, deliverables and acceptance criteria

**Pathway: Agile Product Business Analyst**

A coherent, prioritised MVP product backlog and release recommendation, ready for refinement. No complete FRD, enterprise integration specification or implemented CRM.

These are assignment acceptance criteria used by the assessor. When an output asks you to author solution acceptance criteria, those are your proposed conditions for the business/system/product; they are not supplied answers.

## A1 — Frame the product outcome

**Task:** Frame the product outcome to inform the commissioning decision.

**Submit:** Product goal, outcome measures and product boundary.

**Use:** Sponsor, operational evidence and product feedback. Locate primary records through the release manifest; participant outputs named as inputs are your own work.

**Acceptance criteria (10% of assessment):**

- **A1-AC1:** Connect the goal to evidenced sales/service problems.
- **A1-AC2:** Define measurable outcomes and limitations.
- **A1-AC3:** Distinguish a product outcome from buying CRM software.
- **A1-AC4:** Identify decision owners and scope exclusions.

**Review:** Check the output against every criterion, record unresolved questions and cross-reference related outputs rather than duplicating inconsistent versions.

## A2 — Understand users and opportunities

**Task:** Understand users and opportunities to inform the commissioning decision.

- **A2-AC2:** Identify evidence versus assumptions.
- **A2-AC3:** Explain competing priorities and accessibility questions.

**Review:** Check the output against every criterion, record unresolved questions and cross-reference related outputs rather than duplicating inconsistent versions.

## A4 — Refine the product backlog

**Task:** Refine the product backlog to inform the commissioning decision.

**Submit:** Epics and 12–18 prioritised user stories with acceptance criteria.

**Use:** A1–A3 and source conflicts. Locate primary records through the release manifest; participant outputs named as inputs are your own work.

**Acceptance criteria (25% of assessment):**

- **A4-AC1:** Use stable IDs and explicit user value.
- **A4-AC2:** Write observable acceptance criteria including failure/permission/data-quality cases.
- **A4-AC3:** Split oversized stories and link dependencies.
- **A4-AC4:** Make uncertainty visible with research spikes rather than invented estimates or stakeholder approval.

**Review:** Check the output against every criterion, record unresolved questions and cross-reference related outputs rather than duplicating inconsistent versions.

## Portfolio-wide conditions
All material conclusions must have evidence references or be labelled proposals/assumptions. Preserve raw data, explain transformations and report limitations. Keep diagrams readable and terms/identifiers consistent. Supply an index to every task and identify contributors and AI assistance. Additional documents earn no credit merely for their volume. No completed participant deliverables are provided in the master.
