# Existing sales, forecast and data-handling sources
**Mettelo BA-045 · Stage 3 · Fictional internal policy**
This is source evidence, not an approved future pipeline, conceptual model or decision table.

## Ownership and authority
Sales owns seller practice and opportunity content; Marketing owns campaign/source capture; Finance owns order reconciliation and financial definitions; Service owns delivery-contact handling; relevant data owners/advisers approve record and permitted-use changes. The sponsor's funding decision does not grant blanket migration or contact use.

No signed end-to-end ownership/lifecycle definition exists in the supplied sources. Participants must investigate which authority applies, rather than assume one commercial director settles every rule.

## Existing sales handbook
Discovery/proposal/committed labels are used without consistent exit evidence. Nick's email says a positive buying indication may be committed; Finance's note says an accepted order is needed for booked business. These statements serve different purposes and must not be silently collapsed.

Seller probabilities are judgement inputs. No standard calibration or validated conversion model is supplied. Missing next-action dates are not proof that follow-up occurred; past expected dates may be stale assumptions needing review.

## Forecast and financial definitions
Retain snapshot date, author and input assumptions. An accepted ERP order is distinct from an opportunity stage. Proposed total contract value, accepted order, invoicing and recognised revenue must not be treated interchangeably.

The recognised-revenue policy and full invoice dataset are out of this exercise. No participant may infer revenue simply by multiplying opportunity value by probability or summing won rows.

## Accounts, sites and source history
Account changes need the data owner's review. Similar names or a shared legal registration may indicate related records but do not by themselves establish that sites, history, ownership and contact restrictions can be flattened. Preserve appropriate provenance and traceability of merge/correction decisions.

Marketing asks that source interactions remain distinguishable. No first-touch/last-touch attribution model is approved. A source code identifies recorded interaction, not causal effectiveness or permission for all contact.

## Information use and access
Review context, purpose, contact type and applicable obligations before importing or contacting people. reference_held is evidence status, not a legal conclusion. Event attendance or a business card is not blanket approval for unrelated uses.

Service may need agreed scope and relevant contact restrictions without all private negotiation notes. Supplier defaults are not an approved access model. The retention schedule and detailed vendor terms are not supplied; do not invent a period or retain everything by default.

## Current uncertainty
No future stage definition, authoritative lifecycle, merge policy, complete access model, approved communications or integration criteria are provided. Bring validated proposals with sources, owner questions and acceptance evidence. The supplied rules remain existing source material for participant analysis, not completed BA deliverables.
