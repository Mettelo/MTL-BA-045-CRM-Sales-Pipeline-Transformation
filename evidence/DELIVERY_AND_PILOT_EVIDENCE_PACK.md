# CRM experiment and commercial evaluation evidence
**Mettelo BA-045 · Stage 6 · Compiled/released 24 August**
Isolated synthetic replay; no live customer contact, migration, marketing or order change.

## Setup
Forty historical leads L0701–L0740 were replayed in baseline on 17 August and a SalesCloud A prototype on 19 August, giving 80 paired observations. Two experienced sellers, one marketer and one Service colleague volunteered. Fixed session order, familiar cases and supplier support limit inference.

The prototype may not be the participant's preferred option. It imports synthetic lead/account/opportunity/order data with seeded responses; there is no production real-time ERP connection. Independent acceptance should be prepared before these results, not reverse-engineered afterwards.

These are observations for investigation, not a completed defect catalogue, agreed severity or participant pass/fail.

## Separate weekly follow-up
For the six-seller work group, 10–16 August produced 40 new lead rows, 29 with owners, 14 recorded overdue follow-ups, 4 linked orders and 18 administration hours. The following week had 35 leads, 33 with owners, 9 overdue follow-ups, 3 orders and 25 hours.

These are separate aggregates, not the June sample. Product/source mix changed and an analyst helped assign owners. Missing next-action dates can fall outside overdue reporting. Orders are not recognised revenue. The prototype was isolated; the weekly change cannot establish a live CRM causal benefit. No reliable conversion window, forecast-error study or sustainable cost reduction is supplied.
